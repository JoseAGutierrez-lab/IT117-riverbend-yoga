Our Yoga Class Offerings & Pricing

Weekly Class Types

At Riverbend, every class is grounded in mindfulness, trauma-informed guidance, and posture adaptability. Explore our core weekly offerings below. Select a preferred class and we will remember it for your workshop inquiry.

Gentle Yoga

Designed specifically for beginners, older adults, and individuals recovering from stiffness or chronic joint fatigue. This class focuses on low-impact floor stretches, supported seated positions, gentle joint mobilization, and relaxation techniques.

Vinyasa Flow

A dynamic, breath-synchronized class that links movement with inhale and exhale cycles. Build functional core strength, balance, and mental focus through flowing sequences with multiple modifications demonstrated for all skill levels.

Restorative Yoga

Unwind and release deep muscular tension through restorative relaxation. Utilizing bolsters, blankets, and blocks, you will rest comfortably in passive postures held for 5 to 10 minutes to reset the nervous system and relieve chronic stress.

Private & Tailored Sessions

For students seeking personalized attention, customized mobility routines, or prenatal adjustments, we offer one-on-one private sessions. Private sessions include comprehensive postural evaluations and personalized mindfulness guidance. Contact us via our Events & Spot Request page to schedule your session.
