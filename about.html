About Riverbend Yoga Studio

Our Teaching Philosophy & Instructors

Our instructors believe that yoga is not a performance or an athletic competition; it is a lifetime practice of self-awareness, deep breathing, and personal restoration. We cultivate an inclusive studio environment where all ages, body shapes, and backgrounds are embraced.

Meet Jane Doe, Studio Founder & Lead Instructor

Jane has been practicing yoga for over fifteen years and has been a certified Yoga Alliance instructor since 2015. Specializing in restorative practices, adaptive yoga for seniors, and workplace stress recovery for busy professionals, Jane creates a peaceful, down-to-earth atmosphere where every student feels supported.

Accessibility Accommodations

Riverbend Yoga is committed to full physical and sensory accessibility for all visitors:

  • Our building features step-free street-level entrance access, wide hallways, and fully accessible single-occupancy restrooms.
  • A complete inventory of props—including non-slip bolsters, cork blocks, cotton straps, and sturdy wooden chairs for seated yoga—is available free of charge for every class.
  • Quiet lobby zones and fragrance-free studio rooms ensure comfort for visitors with sensory or chemical sensitivities.
  • Students with visual or hearing needs are encouraged to speak with their teacher prior to class for optimal mat placement near front demonstration areas.

Studio Policies & Etiquette

To preserve a serene, restorative space for all attendees, we ask all guests to observe our community studio policies:

  • Please arrive 10 to 15 minutes before class begins to settle in quietly.
  • Silence all cell phones, smartwatches, and electronic devices before entering the practice space.
  • Remove outdoor footwear and place belongings in the complimentary storage cubbies in our front lobby.
  • Please inform your instructor before class begins of any recent injuries, chronic conditions, or pregnancy so that appropriate modifications can be prepared.
